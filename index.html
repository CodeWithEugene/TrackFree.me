      Privacy fundamentals

      Four ideas that make privacy policies easier to read.

      01 · Collection

      What information leaves your device?

      Names, location, contacts, content, and device details are all examples. Check whether each is necessary for the feature you use.

      02 · Tracking

      Can activity be connected across places?

      Tracking can connect what you do in one service with activity elsewhere, often for advertising or measurement.

      03 · Sharing

      Who else may get the information?

      Policies may name vendors, affiliates, advertisers, payment providers, and legal authorities. Those are different kinds of sharing.

      04 · Metadata

      What surrounds the content itself?

      Even where content is protected, details such as time, device, IP address, and interactions can reveal a lot.
