Privacy policy

We aim to collect as little as possible.

TrackFree is designed to be useful without accounts, behavioural advertising, or a database of visitor profiles.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

In plain language

You can browse the guide without creating an account. If you ask the optional chat assistant a question, that question is processed to provide a reply.

Information we process

The app explorer works without login details. If you use the chat assistant, we process the question you enter and generate a reply. A question about an app already in the catalogue is answered from the reviewed catalogue. If the optional AI fallback is configured, a question about an unknown app may be sent to that AI provider.

What we ask you not to share

Do not enter passwords, payment information, government identifiers, health information, private messages, or other sensitive or confidential details into the assistant.

Retention and service logs

TrackFree does not intentionally create user accounts or retain chat messages as a product feature. The hosting provider may process standard technical logs for security, abuse prevention, and reliability. We will update this page if that practice changes.

Your choices

You can use the app guides without opening the chat assistant. You can also check the official privacy policy for any linked source before following an action. If you have a privacy question about TrackFree itself, contact the project maintainer through the project repository.

Changes to this policy

We will revise this policy when hosting, analytics, storage, or AI providers change. The date at the top tells you when it was last updated.
